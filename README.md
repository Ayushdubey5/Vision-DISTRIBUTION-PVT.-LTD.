# Vision Distribution Pvt. Ltd. (VDPL) – Official Website  
India’s Leading Telecom, Electronics & Government Supply Distribution Company

This repository contains the official website source code for **Vision Distribution Pvt. Ltd. (VDPL)** — a trusted telecom and electronics distribution house with 30+ years of industry leadership, a strong Pan-India footprint, and a diverse business ecosystem that spans retail, manufacturing, brand licensing, B2G procurement, and more.

---

## 🚀 About Vision Distribution Pvt. Ltd.  
Founded in **1994**, VDPL is one of India’s most respected telecom and consumer electronics distribution companies. With **9 offices**, **300+ employees**, and an impressive **₹4500+ Cr turnover (FY 24–25)**, VDPL serves as a strategic partner to global brands and government organizations.

### **Key Highlights**
- 30+ years of distribution excellence  
- Authorized partners for **Apple, Samsung, Oppo, Swiss Military**, and more  
- Strong distribution network across **North, East & Pan India**  
- Successfully executed **₹2900+ Cr government smart device projects**  
- Operates **27+ Mobiliti World retail stores** across Delhi NCR  
- Proud licensee of **Swiss Military** wearables & hearables products  
- OEM manufacturing capability with **70,000+ monthly production capacity**
